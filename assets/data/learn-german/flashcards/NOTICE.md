The reference-card data in this directory contains mechanically transformed entries from:

- **Deutsch–Japanisch FreeDict+WikDict dictionary 2025.11.23**, FreeDict / WikDict / Wiktionary / DBnary, licensed under **CC BY-SA 3.0**: https://download.freedict.org/dictionaries/deu-jpn/2025.11.23/
- **Leipzig Corpora Collection** frequency samples, Universität Leipzig, licensed under **CC BY**: https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de/en/download/German
- **Tatoeba German–Japanese sentence pairs**, licensed under **CC BY 2.0 FR**: https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads

The derived lexical card data is distributed under **CC BY-SA 3.0**. Tatoeba-derived sentence pairs retain **CC BY 2.0 FR** attribution and source sentence IDs. This notice applies to the derived dictionary and sentence data, not automatically to J-Connect application code, page copy, or the original editorial examples.

A1–B1 placement records the earliest attested Goethe PDF among the cited A1/A2/B1 materials; it is a study reference, not certification of a word's minimum level, every sense, or example difficulty. Headword spelling matches do not certify semantic alignment. The PDF text and Goethe examples are not redistributed.

B2/C1/C2 contain original contextual expression exercises with editorial CEFR learning targets, not officially certified per-word bands. Scene-only practical terms may be unclassified. Frequency and fixed per-level quotas are not used for placement. The full legacy audit is available in vocabulary-audit.csv and vocabulary-audit.json. See docs/learn-german-cefr-vocabulary-methodology.md in the repository for reproducible source hashes, limits and the review process.
